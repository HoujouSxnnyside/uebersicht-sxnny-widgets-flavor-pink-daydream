# Code of Conduct

Sxnnyside Project sees people who want to contribute — not races, genders, sexual identities, or political stances. If you have an idea and want to build something, you're welcome here. Do it correctly.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## Reporting

Report violations to `support.sxnnyside@sxnnysideproject.com`. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer, who may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
